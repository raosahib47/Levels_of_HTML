BRIYANI RECIPE

ingredients
instructions
Ingredients
  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 1/2 cup mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, beans)
  • 1/2 cup chicken or tofu (optional)
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ginger paste
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 2 cups water
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons oil or ghee
Instructions
  1. Rinse the basmati rice under cold water until the water runs clear. Soak the rice in water for 30 minutes, then drain.
  2. Heat oil or ghee in a large pan over medium heat. Add sliced onions and sauté until golden brown.
  3. Add minced garlic and ginger paste to the pan and cook for another minute until fragrant.
  4. Add mixed vegetables and chicken or tofu (if using) to the pan. Cook for 5-7 minutes until the vegetables are tender and the chicken is cooked through.
  5. Stir in garam masala, turmeric powder, red chili powder, and salt. Mix well to coat the vegetables and chicken with the spices.
  6. Add drained rice to the pan and stir gently to combine with the vegetable mixture.
  7. Pour in 2 cups of water and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to low, cover the pan, and simmer for 15-20 minutes until the rice is cooked and all the water is absorbed.
  8. Remove from heat and let it sit covered for 5 minutes. Fluff the rice with a fork before serving.
Tip: Serve with raita or salad for a complete meal!
